HOW TO PLAY

THE REALITY SHIFT: Press SPACEBAR or tap SHIFT to phase instantly between Deep Void and Singularity Rift.

DIMENSIONAL PHASING: Obstacles in the alternate dimension appear as faint holograms. You phase straight through them without taking collision damage!

DIMENSION TRAITS:

  • Deep Void (Cyan): High-traction flight, laser fence barricades, high speed multiplier.
  • Singularity Rift (Magenta): Zero-G drift inertia, tumbling asteroids, station debris, gravity wells.

HOSTILE SENTINELS: Hostile UFO saucers flank the corridor with red-orange rim lighting. Shoot them with twin plasma blasters (J / Left-Click) or detonate an EMP (K / Right-Click).

OVERDRIVE BOOST: Chain dodges and collect crystals to charge your Overdrive meter. When full, press W or Shift to enter invulnerable Relativistic Warp Speed!
